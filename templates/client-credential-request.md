## Important

Please do not send:

- Passwords
- OTP codes
- Recovery codes
- Private keys
- Access tokens
- Refresh tokens
- Any other account credentials unless specifically requested by the developer

If you encounter a screen or error that you do not understand, take a screenshot and send it to the developer. Before sending the screenshot, make sure it does not show passwords, private keys, tokens, or other secrets.
